How it works

The same four steps run on every camera frame, in the time between one frame and the next. Every picture here is drawn by the engine, live in your browser, from the sample photo.

  1. 01

    Find the face

    A face landmark model fits a mesh to your face, with a depth for every point. Everything after this is placed from those points and scaled to the distance between your eyes. Drag the face to turn it.

    478points, found again on every frame

  2. 02

    Shape soft masks

    Lips, lids, lash line, brows, cheeks and highlights each get a soft-edged shape of their own. The edges soften in proportion to your face, so they stay soft as you lean in.

  3. 03

    Change the colour, keep the texture

    Every pixel of a lip is a point in colour space: lightness up the middle, hue around it. A flat fill lifts the whole lip toward white. Carmine moves the colour and lets the lightness move only a little, so the lip keeps its own light and shadow.

  4. 04

    Paint it in one pass

    All six products are drawn in a single pass on your graphics card, in the order the sheets were stacked. The whole pipeline takes 26.6 ms a frame on an Apple M1 Pro laptop, about 37.6 frames a second.

Your face never leaves this tab.

No server does the work. The page downloads its files from this site, and from then on everything happens on your device.

  • Runs on your device

    The face model and the colour maths run in your browser, on your own graphics card.

  • Nothing is uploaded

    Camera frames go to the screen and nowhere else. Save photo downloads a PNG to your device, and that is the only copy.

  • Check it yourself

    Once the camera is running, turn off your Wi-Fi. It keeps working, because it no longer needs the network.
